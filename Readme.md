# Sales Performance Dashboard

## Overview
This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of key performance metrics for a business. It tracks sales, shipments, costs, and profit across multiple dimensions to help stakeholders make data-driven decisions.

## Key Features

- **KPI Summary**: Displays high-level key performance indicators (KPIs):

  - **Sales:** Total sales revenue.
  - **Boxes:** Total units sold.
  - **Shipments:** Total shipments made.
  - **Costs:** Total operational costs.
  - **Profit:** Net profit with profit percentage visualization.

- **Profit Analysis:**

  - A doughnut chart highlighting the overall profit percentage.

- **Trends Over Time:**

  - Line chart showing trends in **sales, shipments, costs,** and **profits** by month.

- **Product Analysis:**

  - A detailed table of products with:
   - Sales and profit metrics.
   - Profit margins and LBS%.
   - Interactive progress bars for better visualization.

- **Shipments Analysis:**

  - Histogram of shipment distribution.

- **Interactive Navigation:**

  - Dynamic filtering by regions (e.g., Australia, Canada, India, etc.).
  - Sidebar navigation for easier access to different segments.

## Data Insights

1. **Regional Performance:**

- Track performance across different countries like **Australia, India, UK,** etc.
- Identify top-performing regions.

2. **Product Performance:**

- Analyze product-wise sales and profit margins.
- Highlight underperforming products with high costs or low profit percentages.

3. **Monthly Trends:**

- View how sales, costs, and profits fluctuate over time.
- Identify patterns or anomalies in shipments and costs.

## How to Use

1. **Open the Dashboard:**

- Download the `.pbix file` and open it in Power BI Desktop.

2. **Interactivity:**

- Use the **sidebar navigation** to filter by categories (Bars, Bites, Other).
- Select regions to analyze specific country-wise data.
- Hover over charts for detailed tooltips and insights.

3. **Customization:**

- Connect your own dataset to replicate the structure.
- Modify filters to tailor the dashboard for your business needs.

## Requirements

- **Power BI Desktop** (latest version recommended).
- Dataset containing fields like:
  - Sales, Profit, Costs, Shipments, Product Names, Region, etc.
